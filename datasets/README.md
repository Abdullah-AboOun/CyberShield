# Datasets (not committed)

All dataset files under `datasets/` (except `README.md`) are gitignored. Unit tests build their own small synthetic Zeek rows inline.

## Comparison: Pros, Cons & Notes

| Folder / Dataset | Role | Pros | Cons / Caveats | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| `cic2017_zeek/` (**CIC-IDS2017**, 5d Zeek) | **Primary training & test** | Real Zeek `conn.log` schema; **612k real benign TCP flows** (SSL/HTTP/SSH/FTP); timestamps (`ts`) for 60s windows; benign + 12 attack types mixed on the same LAN. | Single lab (attacker VM hit 1 victim IP → dropped `host_uniq_ips_60s`); 2017 traffic (less QUIC/HTTP3); Thursday Web Attacks mostly empty/failed flows. | 52 GB PCAPs → 48 MB Parquet via [colab_cic2017_pipeline.py](../notebooks/colab_scripts/colab_cic2017_pipeline.py). The Parquet holds CICFlowMeter CSV labels (5-tuple inner join), which have known errors (e.g. ~74k infiltration port-scan flows marked BENIGN). `load_cic2017_zeek()` relabels from UNB's attack schedule (`CIC_ATTACK_SCHEDULE`) and keeps the old ones as `label_csv`. |
| `uwf_zeek/` (**UWF-ZeekData24 / Fall24-2 / Sum25-1 / Sum25-2**) | **Co-training (`24`/`Fall24-2`) & unseen 2025 test (`Sum25-1/2`)** | Native Zeek Parquet from https://datasets.uwf.edu/data/; modern 2024–2025 captures; MITRE ATT&CK labels (Credential Access, Discovery, Recon, Defense Evasion, Lateral Movement, Exfiltration). | **Idle-lab benign traffic** (92–99% UDP DNS, **0-byte** benign TCP); benign and attack captured in separate weeks. | Drop `label_binary == "Duplicate"` multi-tactic rows. Do not train alone for a real LAN—combine balanced chunks with `CIC-IDS2017`. |
| `ait_zeek/` (**AIT-LDSv2** `russellmitchell`, 2022 Zeek) | **Unseen 3rd-network zero-shot test** | Real 2022 enterprise red-team kill chain (OpenVPN internal sweep, WP-Crack, reverse shell, dnsteal); attacker IPs `10.9.0.10` & `192.168.230.122` from AIT scenario config. | The two PCAPs (`attacker_0/logs/{dnsteal,wpdiscuz}/traffic.pcap`) are near-identical captures **on the attacker host**: ~98.5% attack flows, the rest is IPv6/mDNS/loopback noise. **Recall-only test.** | 7.1 GB Zenodo archive → 6.7 MB Parquet via [colab_ait_zeek_pipeline.py](../notebooks/colab_scripts/colab_ait_zeek_pipeline.py). Labeled attack if **either** end is an attacker IP (dnsteal exfil goes *to* the attacker). `M3`: red team 99.7%, DNS exfil 0%. |
| `ait_lds_zeek/` (**AIT-LDSv2.0**, all 8 scenarios, Zeek 9.0.1) | **8 labelled enterprise networks** (`load_ait_lds_zeek()`) | `labelled/<scenario>.parquet`: 2.50M connections from the company capture points (attacker host excluded), one row per connection after merging copies seen by several probes (`n_probes`), trimmed to the simulation window. 631k attacks: DNS exfiltration 296k, host discovery 279k, service scan 30k, dirb 17k, DNS brute force 9k, plus wpscan, webshell, cracking, reverse shell (`attack_type`). Attack share per scenario 7% (shaw) to 58% (russellmitchell). | Our own labels, not AIT's: AIT-NDS labels by time window only, which marks VPN employees as attackers and misses the reverse shell and the internal exfil leg. We match each connection to the **attacker's own capture** (same proto, src port, dst IP/port, within 1 s; src IP ignored because of VPN NAT), and exfil to the firewall's dnsmasq log lines for the exfil domain. Agrees with AIT-NDS on the scans; differs where AIT is wrong (see `ait_lds_crosscheck.py`). Long connections can be split at pcap rotation boundaries (13–31 pcaps per scenario). | 131 GB of zips → `<scenario>_conn.parquet` (raw, all probes) via [colab_ait_lds_full_zeek.py](../notebooks/colab_scripts/colab_ait_lds_full_zeek.py); configs and logs (`meta/`, ~40 MB download) via [ait_lds_fetch_meta.py](../notebooks/colab_scripts/ait_lds_fetch_meta.py); labels via [ait_lds_label.py](../notebooks/colab_scripts/ait_lds_label.py); `ait_nds_labels.parquet` = AIT-NDS flows for the cross-check. |
| `ton_iot/` (**TON_IoT** Network CSV) | **Single-flow reference** | Native Zeek columns (`proto`, `service`, `conn_state`, bytes/pkts); real benign TCP + 9 attack types. | **No `ts` timestamps** in `train_test_network.csv` (cannot build 60s host windows); IoT sensor loop (`src_pkts == 120`); 56% duplicate flows. | Useful for single-flow cross-network sanity checks, not Hybrid 60s window training. |
| `unsw_official/` (**UNSW-NB15**, creators' Zeek logs) | **Used by notebooks 01 and 02** | The creators' own Zeek `conn.log` files from the official download, labelled from the official ground-truth table; 1.92M connections after dropping the broken Day 1 part 11 log. | Normal traffic comes from 10 generator hosts; many UDP attacks don't match a connection; Day 1 part 3 log is missing. | Built by [colab_unsw_official_zeek.py](../notebooks/colab_scripts/colab_unsw_official_zeek.py) (`conn.parquet`, `gt.parquet`, `parts.csv`, `summary.json`). |
| `unsw_zeek/` (**UNSW-NB15**, 20 GB Zeek, mirror) | **Superseded by `unsw_official/`** | Real Zeek conversion of 20 PCAPs (393k flows, 8 attack categories) labeled via `Stime`/`Ltime` GT. | 10 IXIA Normal IPs are busier (**134 conn/min & 58 ports/min**) than the attackers (**32 & 8**), and `M3` flags 50.6% of their flows: it learned "busy host = attack". Labels look under-matched: 1.1% attack vs ~12.6% in full UNSW-NB15, and 1,675 of 6,159 attacker-subnet flows are labeled Normal. | Built from a HuggingFace mirror, not the official download, via [colab_unsw_zeek_pipeline_mirror.py](../notebooks/archive/colab_unsw_zeek_pipeline_mirror.py). Kept as an audit of the volume bias (real LANs have busy benign DNS/proxy/NAS hosts); fix the label join before trusting its numbers. |
| `unsw_nb15/` (**UNSW-NB15** legacy CSVs) | **Legacy baseline only** | Small, easy to load (`UNSW_NB15_training-set.csv`, `testing-set.csv`). | Argus/Bro hybrid (not Zeek `conn_state`); relies on TTL shortcuts; missing `ts` and IPs. | Source: https://research.unsw.edu.au/projects/unsw-nb15-dataset. |
